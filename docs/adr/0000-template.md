# ADR-NNNN: Short title of the decision

- **Status:** Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by ADR-NNNN | Deprecated
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD

## Context

What situation forces a decision? State the problem, the constraints, and the forces in
tension. Reference earlier records where the decision responds to them. Be factual; this
is the part a future reader leans on to judge whether the decision still holds.

## Decision

The decision, stated plainly in the active voice: "We will ...". One decision per record.
Include the specifics that matter (versions, boundaries, what is explicitly in and out of
scope).

## Consequences

What becomes easier and what becomes harder as a result. Include the costs and the
follow-on work the decision creates, not only the benefits. A decision with no downsides
listed is usually a decision not examined closely enough.

## Alternatives considered

The options that were weighed and rejected, each with the reason it lost. This is what
stops the decision from being relitigated from scratch later.
