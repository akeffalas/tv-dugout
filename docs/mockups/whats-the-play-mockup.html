What's the Play? : the question screen

Which way should the field be drawn? Both show the same play: one out, runner on first, ground ball to the shortstop. The child presses a direction to throw.

A. Top-down diamond

Like a coach's whiteboard. The arrows match the bases exactly (up is 2nd, right is 1st), so the remote maps to the field with no thinking.

B. Behind home plate

Looks like a TV broadcast and feels more like a video game, with sky and an outfield wall. The far bases are squashed, so runners and the ball read smaller.

My recommendation is A. It's clearer for an 8-year-old, and the arrows map straight onto the bases. The fun comes from the replay animation (ball flies, runner slides, a big OUT! stamp).
