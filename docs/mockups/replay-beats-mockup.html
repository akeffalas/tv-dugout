Making the replay easy to follow

Play 2: you're the shortstop, 1 out, runner on first, ground ball to you. Press a base under each field to replay it.

A. Today

  • The ball jumps back to home plate when you press
  • Everyone moves at once, at the same steady speed
  • Ball and runner arrive together; the stamp decides
  • Nothing stays on the field afterwards

B. One thing at a time

  • The hit plays when the question appears; the replay starts from you
  • Your base lights up the moment you press
  • Beat 1: the covering player runs to the bag
  • Beat 2: the race. Ball and runner leave chalk lines; the winner arrives first
  • Players not in the play fade back
  • The lines stay for the why. A wrong call stays dashed beside the right one
