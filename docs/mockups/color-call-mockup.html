Dugout TV · game mockup

Color Call

Every pitch comes with a call. Swing only at the called color and let the other two go by. It's the color call drill from home practice, on the TV, with one button.

Call it

Space or Enter (or a click) stands in for the remote's Select button. Do nothing to take the pitch.

Each ball is half white and half color, like the marked practice balls. There's no red or green, since about 1 in 12 boys can't tell those apart.

Call levels

The harder levels follow the plan's progression. Every level is open, and the child picks one on the start screen.

The first game plays levels 1 to 3. Levels 4 and 5 come later. Pitches get a little faster at each level.

Built for the remote

  • One button. Select swings, and doing nothing is a take. There's no aiming and no D-pad during a pitch.
  • It tests the decision, not reflexes. A press anywhere during the flight counts once the call shows, and the bat meets the ball on its own, so remote lag can't cost a point. A press before the call is ignored.
  • Ten pitches a round, then a line score with Play again focused. Back opens the same "leave this round?" check the other game uses.
  • No streaks. The card on Home shows the best round, the same way What's the Play? does.
