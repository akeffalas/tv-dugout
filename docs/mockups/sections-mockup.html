Where do Videos and Games live?

After a child picks their profile. Both leave room for more sections later.

A. Section row at the top of Home (recommended)

Like Google TV's own top menu. Home opens on Videos as it does today, and pressing up then right reaches Games. Adding a section later just adds a word to the row.

B. A "pick what to do" screen

Big friendly tiles right after the profile picker. Each choice is obvious, but every visit takes one extra press before any video, and Back has one more screen to step through.
